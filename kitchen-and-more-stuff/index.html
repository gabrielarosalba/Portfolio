Kitchen and more stuff

A one-page marketplace I built to sell my kitchen things before moving. Bilingual (EN/DE), mobile-first and designed to be dropped into university group chats instead of spamming them with photos.

A few decisions

Bilingual everywhere. My buyers are a mix of international students and German locals, and making either group translate a listing adds more work and time to a 2-euro decision.

The “Ask about item” button opens a pre-written bilingual message with the item name and price, so buyers don’t have to type anything (Recognition rather than Recall)

Telegram instead of WhatsApp or a form, to keep my phone number private.

Sold items disappear. Reserved items stay visible with a status label, since Reserved sometimes falls through; keeping it listed means the next person can still ask.

No cart, no checkouts, no accounts since it would have been an overhead for a handful of items sold to people picking them up in person.

On the build..

I sketched the layout in Figma first, then built it in a day with Claude Code, partly to see what I could and couldn’t do with it. The interesting limit was: live updates would have meant a database, so instead I update items through an admin view and commit the exported HTML... good enough for this volume, also allowing me to upload them slowly. This was a reminder that the right amount of infrastructure is usually less than it first looks.

Still a work in progress. I keep finding responsive edge cases and fixing them as they come up.
